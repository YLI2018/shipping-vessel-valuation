# Sample data

Anonymised data used by the published models. Only sales reported on or before the valuation date of 22 May 2026 are included. Ship names, dates, buyers, sellers and broker details are not published.

- `comparables.csv`: the 43 MR tanker sales used by the M1 age curve (Sale 01–43): age at sale and price adjusted to 22 May 2026 (US$m).
- `hedonic_sales.csv`: the 297 tanker and bulker sales used by M1+ (H001–H297): segment, type, age (years), deadweight, yard country, survey due within 12 months, survey date unknown, scrubber, eco, en bloc, quarter of the report, reported price (US$m).
